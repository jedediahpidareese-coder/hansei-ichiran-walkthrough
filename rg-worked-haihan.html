40  The abolition decree （廃藩置県の詔）

imperial proclamation (太政官布告) reproduced in a biography, paired with a government chronicle entry (『明治史要』本紀) · 明治4年7月14日 / 1871-08-29

This chapter pairs the two contemporary records of the 29 August 1871 abolition of the domains (廃藩置県): the imperial proclamation itself, reproduced in Inoue Kaoru’s biography, and the government’s own chronicle, 『明治史要』, which records who was summoned and commended that day. The proclamation is the richest 文語 passage in the whole source set — in a few lines it stacks 惟フニ (“on reflection”), a 未然形＋ば hypothetical (欲セハ), the honorific-verb string 相副ヒ … 歸セシムヘシ (causative しむ ＋ obligation べし), the humble 奉セシム, and the negated adnominal 舉ラサル者 (ず negation, 連体形) — exactly the classical machinery an N2 reader has never been drilled on, all in service of a single argument. The chronicle’s commendation clause is also the guide’s showcase verification example: an earlier machine transcription fabricated five “Tokugawa” cadets here, when the source in fact names FOUR lords from four different families (徳川・細川・池田・蜂須賀 = Tokugawa / Hosokawa / Ikeda / Hachisuka). Learning to read the genbun is what lets you catch that error.

The 未然形＋ば = a HYPOTHETICAL 'if', NOT modern 已然形＋ば (which is 'because/when'). Read the whole chunk as 「〜しようとするならば」.せんとほっせば、
書き下し
内（うち）以（もっ）て億兆（おくちょう）を保安し、外（そと）以て万国（ばんこく）と対峙（たいじ）せんと欲（ほっ）せば、
現代語訳
国内では万民の安全を守り、国外では世界の国々と対等に張り合おうとするならば、
English
if we are to safeguard the millions at home and stand on a par with the nations of the world abroad,
原文
宜クよろしくit is fitting that; by all means ought to現代語: 当然〜すべきだ／ぜひとも。文語 副詞 宜く, part of the pattern 「宜く…べし」= 'ought properly to …'. Pairs with 歸セシムヘシ at the end.よろしく名實めいじつname and substance; form and reality現代語: 名目と実態／建前と実質。旧字体 實=実. The edict's core theme: the daimyō held the NAME (知藩事) without the SUBSTANCE of central control.めいじつ相副ヒあいそいcorrespond to each other; accompany one another現代語: 互いに一致して／伴って。相 = 'mutually' (as in 相談); 副ふ (連用形 副ひ) = to accompany/match. 旧仮名 ヒ = い. So 'name and substance mutually match'.あいそい、政令せいれいgovernment decrees; political authority現代語: 政治上の命令／政治権力。Bureaucratic term: the issuing of political orders, i.e. sovereign administrative power.せいれい一ニ歸セシムヘシいつにきせしむべしmust be made to converge on one (center)現代語: 一つのところに帰するようにさせるべきだ／統一しなければならない。SHOWCASE form. 歸す(帰す, converge) 未然形 歸せ + しむ (causative, =modern させる) 終止形 しむ + べし (obligation, =なければならない). So 歸セシムヘシ = 「帰せさせるべきだ／統一させねばならない」. 旧字体 歸=帰.いつにきせしむべし。
書き下し
宜（よろ）しく名実（めいじつ）相（あい）副（そ）ひ、政令（せいれい）一（いつ）に帰（き）せしむべし。
現代語訳
当然、名（たてまえ）と実（実態）とが互いに一致し、政治の命令は一つのところに帰するようにさせなければならない。
English
name and substance ought to correspond, and political authority must be made to converge on a single center.
原文
朕嚢ニさきにpreviously; earlier現代語: 先に／以前に。文語 副詞, read さきに = modern 「先に／かつて」. The source prints 嚢; the word intended here is the classical 曩（さきに, 'formerly'), so 嚢 is best read as a scan/printing variant for 曩 — do NOT take it in its literal sense 'bag/sack'. Transcribed as printed in the source.さきに諸藩版籍奉還はんせきほうかんthe 1869 'return of the land and population registers' to the throne現代語: 版籍奉還（1869年の土地・人民の朝廷返上）。Legal/historical term. 版=land, 籍=population registers, 奉還=respectfully returning. The step BEFORE haihan-chiken.はんせきほうかんノ議ぎproposal; deliberation現代語: 提案／建議。Here = the formal proposal put to the throne.ぎヲ聴納シちょうのうしheeded; accepted (a petition)現代語: 聞き入れて／受け入れて。Bureaucratic verb 聴納す = 'listen and take in', i.e. a superior granting a petition. 連用形 聴納し.ちょうのうし、新ニ知藩事ちはんじdomain governor (the office ex-daimyō held 1869–71)現代語: 知藩事（1869–71年の旧大名の官職名）。Meiji office title: after 版籍奉還 the daimyō were reappointed as salaried 'governors' of their own former domains.ちはんじヲ命シ、各其職ヲ奉セシムほうぜしむhad (them) serve in / discharge (the office)現代語: 務めさせる／奉じさせる。奉ず (to serve/discharge, humble nuance) 未然形 奉ぜ + しむ (causative =させる), sentence-final 終止形 しむ. So 「奉ぜしむ」=「（各自にその職を）奉じさせた／務めさせた」. Same causative しむ as in the previous clause.ほうぜしむ。
書き下し
朕（ちん）嚢（さき）に諸藩（しょはん）版籍奉還（はんせきほうかん）の議を聴納（ちょうのう）し、新（あら）たに知藩事（ちはんじ）を命じ、各（おのおの）其（そ）の職を奉（ほう）ぜしむ。
現代語訳
私は先に、諸藩による版籍奉還の申し出を聞き入れ、新たに知藩事を任命して、それぞれにその職務を務めさせた。
English
I previously heeded the various domains' proposal for the return of the registers, newly appointed governors, and had each take up that office.
原文
然ルニしかるにhowever; and yet現代語: しかし／ところが。文語 接続詞 = modern 「しかし・ところが」. Marks the pivot from 'I did X' to 'but a problem remains'.しかるに數百年因襲いんしゅうinherited custom; long-standing convention現代語: 古くからの慣習／しきたり。Vocabulary: 因（依る）+ 襲（受け継ぐ）= custom handed down. Here = the entrenched Tokugawa-era domain system.いんしゅうノ久キひさしきlong duration; being long-standing現代語: 長く続いていること／長い年月。形容詞 久し, 連体形 久き (nominalized) = 'the long-continuing (state of)'. 旧仮名表記 久キ = 久しき.ひさしき、或ハ其名アリテそのなありてthe name exists, and yet現代語: その名はあって／名目はあっても。あり (文語 'to exist', modern ある) 連用形 あり + て. Pairs with the next 其實舉ラサル in a 'name vs. substance' contrast.そのなありて其實舉ラサル者あがらざるものcases in which (the substance) is not realized現代語: 上がらない者／実効の伴わないもの。KEY negation form. 舉る(挙る=is realized/raised) 未然形 舉ら + ず(negation) 連体形 ざる + 者. ず/ざる = modern ない, but the 連体形 is ざる, not ない. 旧仮名 サル = ざる. 旧字体 舉=挙. So 舉ラサル者 = 「上がらない者・実の伴わないもの」.あがらざるものアリ。
書き下し
然（しか）るに数百年因襲（いんしゅう）の久（ひさ）しき、或（ある）いは其（そ）の名ありて其の実（じつ）挙（あ）がらざる者あり。
現代語訳
しかしながら、数百年続いた古い慣習が長く根づいているために、中には名目だけあって実質の上がらない（実効の伴わない）ものもある。
English
But with the long persistence of several hundred years of inherited custom, in some cases the name exists while the substance is not realized.
原文
…[ellipsis — text elided in the source quotation]現代語: （中略）。The source file itself prints … here: material between the previous clause and this one is omitted in the quoted excerpt. Not a gap in your reading — do not try to supply it. 仍テよってtherefore; accordingly現代語: よって／そこで。文語 接続詞 仍て = modern 「よって・したがって」. Marks the operative conclusion of the edict. 旧仮名 テ = て.よって今更ニいまさらにnow, anew現代語: いま改めて／ここで新たに。Here literally 'now once more/afresh' — NOT the modern idiom 「今さら（=too late now）」. Read as 「いま改めて」.いまさらに藩ヲ廢シはいしabolish現代語: 廃止して。サ変 廃す, 連用形 廃し. 旧字体 廢=廃. This is the 廃藩 of 廃藩置県.はいし縣ト爲スけんとなすmake (them) into prefectures現代語: 県とする／県にする。爲す(為す)=to make into. 旧字体 縣=県, 爲=為. 書き下し
…仍（よっ）て今（いま）更（さら）に藩を廃（はい）し県（けん）と為（な）す。…
現代語訳
…そこで、いま改めて藩を廃止し、県とする。…
English
… Therefore I now abolish the domains and make them prefectures. …
原文
汝なんじyou現代語: お前／あなたがた。文語 second-person pronoun, addressed by a superior to inferiors. Here the emperor to the assembled officials.なんじ群臣ぐんしんthe assembled ministers/officials現代語: 多くの臣下／諸官。群（多くの）+ 臣（臣下）. The officials and lords receiving the edict.ぐんしん其レそれ(emphatic particle) — 'now then', 'do'現代語: （強調の語）どうか／まさに。文語 其れ used as an emphatic/hortatory adverb before a command, not the pronoun 'that'. Reinforces the imperative that follows.それ朕カ意ちんがいmy (the emperor's) intent現代語: 私（天皇）の意図。カ here = classical possessive 「が」 (が written カ in katakana okurigana), so 朕カ意 = 「朕が意」= my intent.ちんがいヲ體セヨたいせよembody it; take it to heart and act on it現代語: 体せよ／よく理解して実行せよ。サ変 體す(体す) 命令形 體せよ = modern 「体せよ・しろ」. 命令形. 旧字体 體=体. Closes the edict with a direct order.たいせよ。
書き下し
汝（なんじ）群臣（ぐんしん）其（そ）れ朕（ちん）が意（い）を体（たい）せよ。
現代語訳

English
You, my ministers — embody my intent.
原文
十四日詔シテみことのりして／しょうしてissuing an imperial decree現代語: 詔（天皇の命令）を下して。詔 = an imperial pronouncement; as a サ変 verb 詔す, 連用形+て 詔して = 'by decreeing'. … 各藩知事ヲ御前ニ召シごぜんにめしsummoned before the imperial presence現代語: 天皇の前に呼び出して。御前 = 'before (the emperor)'; 召す (召し, 連用形) = honorific 'to summon'. English

原文
又またalso; moreover現代語: また／さらに。Opens the SECOND commendation group. Crucial to the reading: it separates a distinct set of four lords from the first four.また徳川慶勝、細川護久、池田慶徳、蜂須賀茂韶ノ建議けんぎa formal proposal / memorial submitted to a superior現代語: 上申・提案。Bureaucratic term: a policy proposal put up to the government/throne.けんぎヲ立纒シりってんしdrew together and gave order to (their proposals)現代語: 取りまとめて筋道を立てて。纒 = 新字体 纏 (まとめる). 立纒 = 'set up and bind together', i.e. consolidate the proposals into a coherent plan. サ変 連用形 立纒し. (This compound is rare; reading りってん follows the corrected source, not an independent dictionary attestation.)りってんし、其當ヲ得テそのとうをえてand being apt / found to be to the point現代語: その適切さ（道理にかなうこと）を得て。當(当) here = 'aptness, fitting the case'. 得て = 'having obtained'. Uncertain readings in this passage

  • SHOWCASE AUDIT LESSON (from the source file’s own header): an earlier machine transcription of the commendation clause fabricated FIVE ‘Tokugawa’ cadets — rendering the names as 「徳川徳成、(徳)川徳久、(徳)川徳達、(徳)川徳[N]、蜂須賀茂韶」 with invented ‘Toku-’ given names and a spurious fifth entry. The corrected, verified reading is FOUR lords from FOUR different families: 徳川慶勝 (Tokugawa Yoshikatsu, Owari — gosanke), 細川護久 (Hosokawa Morihisa, Kumamoto — tozama), 池田慶徳 (Ikeda Yoshinori, Tottori), 蜂須賀茂韶 (Hachisuka Mochiaki, Tokushima — tozama). The lesson: 又 opens a second, distinct group, and 徳川 is one surname among four, not a family label covering all of them. Reading the genbun is what catches the fabrication. (This checker independently confirmed all four names appear verbatim in the .qmd source, with no fabricated fifth entry.)
  • Two further small corrections the source file records for this clause: the prior transcription had 「嘉嘉ス」 (doubled) — corrected to 「嘉ス」; and 「當ノ得テ」 — corrected to 「其當ヲ得テ」. The forms glossed above follow the corrected 2026-05-26 reading, and match the .qmd verbatim.
  • The character 嚢 in 「朕嚢ニ…」 (clause 4) is transcribed AS PRINTED in the source .qmd. 嚢 literally means ‘bag/sack’; the word intended in this idiom is the classical 曩（さきに, ‘formerly, previously’). The reading さきに and meaning ‘previously’ are certainly correct for the sense; 嚢 is best treated as a scan/printing variant (or OCR-adjacent misprint) for 曩. If a paper section is made load-bearing on this exact character, cross-check against another edition of the Inoue biography or the 法令全書 text of the rescript. (The original draft’s token note asserted 嚢 IS the ‘formerly’ character; softened here to flag the 嚢/曩 discrepancy.)
  • The proclamation quotation (clauses 1–7) is reproduced in the .qmd from the Inoue biography and contains printed ellipses (…) at two points (before 仍テ and before 汝群臣). Those … mark text ELIDED in the source excerpt, not gaps in transcription; the elided material is not supplied here and should not be invented. (The single ASCII space the source prints between clause 6’s trailing … and clause 7’s 汝群臣 is a formatting separator, not a dropped character.)
  • Register caveat: 詔 vs 勅 are both rendered loosely as ‘imperial decree/word’ in English but differ (詔 = formally promulgated decree; 勅 = the emperor’s personal command to those present). The distinction is preserved in the glosses but flagged here because English collapses it. Note also that the chronicle text (clauses 8–10) is Meiji Shiyō’s summary of the event, NOT the verbatim imperial decree; the verbatim decree text is the proclamation in clauses 1–7 (Inoue biography), and the full 太政官布告 is in 法令全書, not in this workspace.
  • 文語 form disambiguations to keep honest: in 首唱セシヲ the し is the past auxiliary き (連体形), not a サ変 連用形; and 舉ラサル者 is ず-negation 連体形 ざる, read ‘the not-realized ones’. If a reader is unsure whether a given …し is ‘past’ or ‘and (連用形)’, context (a following noun/を favors past 連体形) decides. These are marked as reasoned readings, not certainties, per the source’s transcription discipline.
  • The workspace transcription notes that a handful of characters in the p.168-R continuation are partially obscured in the white-on-black scan; the four commendation names are legible in the corrected reading, but the file flags that any paper section made load-bearing on exact name characters should be cross-checked against 公文録 / 太政官日誌. The 立纒 compound in particular is rare and its reading (りってん) follows the corrected source rather than an independent dictionary attestation.
  • Vocabulary/kanji-pair scope note: 大參事・秩祿・大藏省 (and the old-form pairs 藏→蔵, 參→参, 舊→旧, 祿→禄, 經→経) come from the chronicle CONTINUATION on p.168-R, which is quoted in the source-transcription .md but NOT in this chapter’s genbun clauses. They are retained as supplementary vocabulary/kanji reference and labeled as such, not as an error.
