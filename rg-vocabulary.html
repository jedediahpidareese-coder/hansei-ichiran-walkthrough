38  Bureaucratic & legal vocabulary

Meiji government prose runs on a compact, recurring vocabulary of statecraft — imperial speech-acts, administrative offices, ranks, and the fixed formulas of the petition style. Once these are familiar, the documents read much more quickly, because the same few dozen words carry most of the institutional weight.

This glossary is built automatically from the worked passages in this section, so every entry is a word you will actually meet here. For the fixed 候文 (petition-style) formulas — 奉存候, 被仰付候, 恐惶敬白 — see the debt-petition chapter, where they are read in context.

語 読み 意味
位階 いかい court rank (e.g. 従五位) — the pre-existing rank listed beside the newly granted peerage in the roster
一代 いちだい ‘for one generation/life’ — the life-only duration-category of a stipend
一等 いっとう first grade / first rank on the numbered official-grade (官等) ladder
因襲 いんしゅう inherited custom; entrenched long-standing convention
右院 ういん the U-in — the chamber where the heads and vice-heads of the ministries deliberated
永禁錮 えいきんこ permanent/indefinite imprisonment
榮爵 えいしゃく honored/glorious rank
永世 えいせい ‘in perpetuity’ — the hereditary duration-category of a stipend
大藏省 おおくらしょう the Ministry of Finance, tasked here with drawing up ranks and stipends; source continuation p.168-R
億兆 おくちょう the whole populace; the myriad people (lit. ‘a hundred million, a trillion’)
各 おのおの each; respectively (marks that a listed sum applies to every named person)
御中 おんちゅう addressee honorific for an office or organization (still used today)
下賜 かし to confer downward — a grant bestowed by the sovereign/state upon a subject
嘉ス かす to commend / approve with praise (superior of inferior); 漢文訓読 register
華族 かぞく the peerage class (former daimyō and court nobles) created in the post-1871 status reordering
家祿 かろく hereditary family stipend paid to former daimyō/samurai households by the central treasury after the 1871 abolition of domains
官制 かんせい the formal system of government offices and their ranks; the administrative structure being revised here
管内 かんない within one’s administrative jurisdiction (the governed domain)
官報 かんぽう the official government gazette in which laws and decrees were promulgated
雅樂 ががく gagaku, the traditional court music; here the name of a court-music bureau placed in the Sei-in
聞食届 きこしめしとどけ honorific verb for the sovereign graciously hearing and granting a request
恐惶敬白 きょうこうけいはく fixed formal sign-off of an epistolary petition: ‘with fear and reverence, respectfully stated’
局 きょく a bureau — an internal sub-office of a ministry or chamber
謹愼 きんしん confinement as punishment; enforced house arrest / self-restraint
金禄公債證書 きんろくこうさいしょうしょ hereditary-stipend public-debt bond certificate — the government bond issued in 1876 in lieu of future stipend payments
議員 ぎいん appointed member (of a chamber); here not an elected legislator
議長 ぎちょう the Speaker / presiding officer of a chamber
下渡ス くだしわたす to hand down / disburse from the state to a recipient
勳冑 くんちゅう meritorious lineages; descendants of the meritorious (source prints 冑 U+5191; the lineage sense proper to 胄 U+80C4)
群臣 ぐんしん the assembled ministers and officials
繼嗣 けいし heir; the successor to the household headship
卿等 けいら the sovereign’s form of address to high officials (‘you lords/ministers’)
削ル けずる to cut / reduce (here, to reduce a domain’s assessed kokudaka)
建議 けんぎ a formal proposal or memorial submitted to a superior authority
現石 げんこく the current/actual assessed rice yield used as the fiscal base, distinct from the nominal 草高 kokudaka
公債 こうさい public debt / government bond
更始 こうし a fresh start; renewal (of the state) — the Restoration framed as beginning anew
更定 こうてい to revise and re-establish (a system); to fix anew
石 こく koku; the unit of a domain’s assessed rice yield (kokudaka), used to express fief size (formal on’yomi of numerals: 四=シ, e.g. 四万石=しまんごく)
御沙汰書 ごさたしょ a document conveying an official ruling/decision handed down from the throne or the Council of State
五爵 ごしゃく the Five Ranks: 公侯伯子男 (prince, marquess, count, viscount, baron)
權（権） ごん ‘provisional / acting / deputy’ — a prefix marking an office one grade below the full holder (e.g. 權大史 = deputy Daishi)
採容 さいよう acceptance/adoption of a petition by the authority
左院 さいん the Sa-in — the legislative / law-drafting and advisory chamber
参議 さんぎ sangi — a councillor of state; the senior collegial policy post directly under the Grand Minister
死一等ヲ減ズ しいっとうをげんず to commute a death sentence by one degree (a set phrase of the penal code)
士族 しぞく the former-samurai legal class
首唱 しゅしょう to be the first to advocate / propose something
殊典 しゅてん special honor / exceptional dispensation
詔勅 しょうちょく an imperial rescript/decree — the document type of this passage
賞典祿 しょうてんろく merit/reward stipend granted for service in the 1868 Restoration
諸官省 しょかんしょう all the various government offices and ministries collectively
從五位 じゅごい Junior Fifth Rank — a court rank (位階), a ladder distinct from the peerage (爵位)
授爵 じゅしゃく conferral of a peerage title
叙 じょ to confer/arrange in rank (as in 叙爵, 叙位)
條例 じょうれい ordinance / detailed implementing regulation attached to a proclamation (not a modern municipal ‘bylaw’ in this Meiji context)
正院 せいいん the Sei-in — the central executive chamber, top of the new three-chamber Daijōkan, seat of the Grand Minister and councillors
政令 せいれい government decrees / political authority; the issuing of sovereign orders
奏請 そうせい to petition the throne for approval (of an appointment or succession)
對峙 たいじ to stand opposed on equal footing; to confront (the Powers) as a peer
退老 たいろう forced retirement / enforced abdication of the household headship
大勞 たいろう great meritorious service rendered to the state (勞 = merit, not fatigue)
第一條 だいいちじょう Article 1 — the 第〜條 numbering is the structural signature of legal-ordinance style
大參事 だいさんじ senior domain advisor — a former-han office dismissed on this day (appears in the source’s chronicle continuation, p.168-R)
大史 だいし Daishi — a senior secretary-grade official rank within the reorganized structure
太政官 だじょうかん the Daijōkan — the supreme council of state and top central-government organ of the early Meiji government (1868–1885)
太政官日誌 だじょうかんにっし the official gazette of the early Meiji government, in which such petitions and their approvals were published
太政官布告 だじょうかんふこく Daijōkan (Grand Council of State) proclamation — the highest-tier legal instrument of pre-1885 Japan, equivalent to a modern statute; superseded by 勅令/法律 after the Cabinet system replaced the Daijōkan in 1885
太政大臣 だじょうだいじん Grand Minister of State — the highest single office in the Daijōkan
秩 ちつ to set in graded order; the graded hierarchy itself (cf. 秩序); サ変 秩す = ちつす
秩祿 ちつろく official stipend / hereditary salary (of samurai and officials); source continuation p.168-R
知藩事 ちはんじ domain governor — the salaried office ex-daimyō held 1869–71 over their former domains
中興 ちゅうこう restoration/revival of the state — here the Meiji Restoration
忠貞 ちゅうてい loyalty and steadfast integrity
寵光 ちょうこう imperial favor; the sovereign’s grace toward a subject
聴納 ちょうのう to heed and accept (a petition); a superior granting a request
勅 ちょく the emperor’s personal command/word (as spoken to those before him)
朕 ちん the Emperor’s exclusive first-person pronoun (‘We/I’); marks the text as the sovereign’s own voice
統括 とうかつ to oversee and control as a single whole; to centralize supervision
黨與 とうよ faction, confederates, a band of accomplices sharing guilt
特旨 とくし special imperial intent; a decision made by the emperor’s particular grace
任ス（任ず） にんず to appoint (to office); classical 文語 サ変 form of modern 任じる/任命する
年限 ねんげん term / fixed duration limit governing how long a stipend runs
廃藩置県 はいはんちけん ‘abolish the domains, establish prefectures’ — the 1871 reform this passage enacts
伯爵 はくしゃく Count — the third of the Five Ranks
版籍奉還 はんせきほうかん the 1869 ‘return of land and population registers’ by the daimyō to the throne (the step before abolition)
藩知事 はんちじ domain governor — the office (1869–1871) into which former daimyō were appointed after 版籍奉還, before the domains were abolished
藩屏 はんぺい bulwark; a protective ‘hedge/screen’ shielding the throne (metaphor for the loyal elite)
布告 ふこく official proclamation / promulgation; the public act (and document-type) of announcing a Daijōkan law
付與 ふよ to confer / bestow / grant (the bonds)
平民 へいみん commoner — the third post-1871 legal class (farmers, merchants, artisans)
別紙 べっし the attached document (here, the appended ordinance)
辨官 べんかん the Bengan, senior secretariat of the 太政官 (Dajōkan / Council of State); the addressee of the petition
封土 ほうど fief; the domain land granted to a daimyō
没ス ぼっす to confiscate (property/land); 封土ヲ没ス = to confiscate the fief
詔 みことのり／しょう an imperial decree / pronouncement (formal, promulgated)
民撫恤 みんぶじゅつ relief and succor of the common people; poor-relief (the stated reallocation target)
名實 めいじつ name and substance; form vs. reality — the edict’s central diagnosis
優列 ゆうれつ the higher ranks; the superior tier of the status order
翼贊 よくさん to assist/lend support to a great undertaking
輪郭附 りんかくづき ‘with border’ — printer’s designation that the decree was set inside the official ruled frame reserved for formal proclamations
列藩 れっぱん the various domains collectively
