36  Old kanji (旧字体 → 新字体)

Before the 1946 spelling reform (当用漢字), many kanji were written in fuller, older forms. You already know the modern character — you only have to recognise its older shape. The pattern is usually a fuller radical or an un-simplified component: 廣→広, 學→学, 鐵→鉄.

This table gathers every 旧字体 form that appears in the worked passages of this section, paired with its modern equivalent. It is built automatically from those passages, so it grows as passages are added.
